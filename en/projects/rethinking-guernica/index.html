Rethinking Guernica

https://guernica.museoreinasofia.es/en
Web Application
Drupal 10
Drupal 8

Migration of the web application Rethinking Guernica from Drupal 8 to Drupal 10 and updating dependencies such as modules, packages, and the Apache Solr search system.

Each of the events documented on this platform illustrates a particular mode of intervention by artists and collectives in different yet related systems and contexts. The proposal "Counter-impossible (im)archives" aims to address this extensive constellation through a questioning of the notion of the archive as a universal and exhaustive repository of documents and information, inviting reflection on different scenarios and relationships, on contingencies and causalities present in every narrative, and to do so not as passive recipients of information, but as active and self-reflective interpreters.
