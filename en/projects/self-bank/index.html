Self Bank

https://www.selfbank.es
Web Application
Drupal 10

Collaboration with the back-end team in the renovation of the web based on Drupal decoupled from Selfbank, a product of the company Singular Bank.

The most unique thing about Singular Bank is its clients. Each has their own life goals and their own way of managing their finances and investments. Some clients want the help of a personal banker who, supported by a team of specialists, advises them on all aspects of their wealth; others prefer to make their own decisions; and some seek both. We never try to adapt the client to the bank, but the bank to the client. At Singular Bank, every client has his or her bank.
