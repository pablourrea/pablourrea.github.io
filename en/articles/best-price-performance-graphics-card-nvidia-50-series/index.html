The best price-to-performance graphics card in the NVIDIA 50 series

Hardware
Graphics Cards

NVIDIA GeForce RTX 50 Series (Source: https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/graphics-cards/50-series)

Pablo Urrea

5 minutes reading
Published
19/01/2025

In early January 2025, during CES 2025 in Las Vegas and with nearly half a year of delay, Jensen Huang, the CEO of NVIDIA, unveiled the new series of gaming-dedicated graphics cards: The NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5080, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 Ti, and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070. Promising the 5070 will offer performance equal to the NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090.

 

Is it worth upgrading to a 50 series graphics card? Since the announcement of the 50 series, I have been analyzing them in order to upgrade my system. Is it worth upgrading your graphics card to one from the 50 series? The only correct answer is “Only if your favorite games stop working”. Based on that, which graphics card should I buy? The answer is quite ambiguous since it depends on the system you want to build. Ignoring Reddit comments and YouTube experts, if you want the best gaming rig, the most powerful graphics card is the 5090, and if you want the cheapest, the 5070 is your graphics card, period, considering these limits and adjusting the budget to each price. Most gamers limit their card to the budget because, indeed, not many people have the latest model. According to Steam in a survey conducted in December 2024, this is the ranking of the top 10 most used graphics cards by its users:

  1. NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060
  2. NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU
  3. NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060
  4. NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650
  5. NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Ti
  6. NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Ti
  7. NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070
  8. NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050
  9. NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Laptop GPU
  10. NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060

Steam Hardware and Software Survey: December 2024

 

We know that the most important thing is not having the latest model, but if you still want to have the most recent one with the idea that it will take longer to become obsolete, let me tell you which model is the most cost-effective of the entire series.

This article is merely a guideline based on the analysis I've done to decide which graphics card from the NVIDIA GeForce RTX 50 series to buy.

 

Which 50 series graphics card has the best price-to-performance ratio? Budget is probably the most important factor when buying a new 50 series graphics card, and that's why we need to look at their prices:

The results show a tie for first place in price-to-performance ratio between the GeForce RTX 5070 Ti and the GeForce RTX 5070, with the one that best matches expectations and/or budget being the better option. In second place is the GeForce RTX 5080, the best option for a balance between power and price. And finally, the GeForce RTX 5090, with the highest price and power of the entire 50 series; if you just want the best, this is the graphics card to choose.
