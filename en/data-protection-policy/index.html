Data Protection Policy

 

Pablo Urrea provides you through the website pablourrea.github.io with this data protection policy in order to inform you, in detail, about how we treat your personal data and protect your privacy and the information you provide us. In case of future modifications to it, we will notify you through the website or through other means so that you can know the new privacy conditions introduced.

In compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679, General Data Protection Regulation and Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and guarantee of digital rights, we inform you of the following:

 

1. Minors

In accordance with data protection regulations, Pablo Urrea determines as an essential requirement for you to access this website that the user is over 14 years old. If you are under 14 years old and have accessed this website, you should not provide us with any of your data. The responsibility for the truthfulness of this information lies with you. Pablo Urrea, in the event of verifying that this requirement is not met, has the authority to disregard the request for information sent. Under no circumstances will data be collected from minors regarding the professional, economic, or privacy situation of other family members without their consent.

 

2. Who is the Data Controller?

Pablo Urrea email address: paurca@gmail.com.

 

3. What information do we collect and for what purpose?

At Pablo Urrea, we process the personal information that the user provides us with directly. In general, we will process identifying data (such as name, surname, ID number, telephone, postal address, email, IP address).

The user guarantees the authenticity, accuracy, and truthfulness of all the information provided to us, committing to keeping the personal data updated so that it always corresponds to their real situation. The User will be solely responsible for false or inaccurate statements and for the damages they may cause.

The personal data that you provide us within the framework of the provision of our services are mandatory and are the minimum necessary for us to manage your order.

The data you provide us may be used for one or more of the purposes that we will indicate below, which will be determined by factors such as your relationship with Pablo Urrea or by the means used to submit the data. Below, we present the treatments referred to in this privacy policy, without prejudice to others for which you will be informed or requested the appropriate authorization:

  1. Compliance with legal obligations: It may be necessary to process personal data in order to comply with the corresponding legal requirements. Specifically, to comply with legislation on data protection, tax, statistics, insurance, etc.
  2. Communication-contact: The data you send us by email, instant messaging applications, or making a claim are used to respond to the request made.
  3. Sending commercial communications by any means with additional consent: If you are our client or explicitly consent, your data may be transferred to collaborating entities for the purpose of informing you by any means for promotional and advertising purposes about services, products, or events organized or participated by us.

 

4. How do we obtain your personal data?

We collect your personal information through different means, but you will always be informed at the time of collection through informative clauses about the data controller, the purpose, as well as how you can exercise your rights in data protection matters.

In general, the personal information we process is limited to identifying data for contact purposes (name and surname, telephone and email), contracted services, and payment and billing data.

Through our website, we collect personal information related to your browsing through the use of Cookies or other storage devices. To clearly and precisely understand the Cookies we use, their purposes, and how you can configure or disable them, please consult our Cookie Policy.

 

5. How long will we keep your data?

We only keep your information for the period of time necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, to comply with the legal obligations imposed on us, and to address any liabilities that may arise from fulfilling the purpose for which the data was collected. Your personal data will be kept for the duration of the legal and/or contractual relationship and, subsequently, as long as you have not exercised your right to deletion, they will be kept in accordance with the legal deadlines applicable in each specific case, taking into account the type of data and the purpose of the processing.

In the event that you exercise a deletion right and it is appropriate, in compliance with article 32 LOPDGDD, it will be blocked, being available only at the request of Judges and Courts, the Ombudsman, the Public Prosecutor's Office, or the competent Public Administrations during the limitation period of the actions that may arise, and, once this period has elapsed, they will be completely deleted.

We process your data lawfully, fairly, transparently, adequately, relevantly, limitedly, accurately, and updated. Therefore, we undertake to take all reasonable measures to ensure that they are deleted or rectified without delay when they are inaccurate.

 

7. What is the legal basis for processing your data?

The legal basis for processing your data is:

  • Contact or commercial communications: your express consent given by accepting this data protection policy before sending the data collection form, when sending us an email or your CV.

In the event that third-party data is provided, you declare that you have the consent of the data subjects for said data communication or, as the case may be, hold their legal representation, exempting Pablo Urrea from any liability.

 

8. What types of data do we process?

The categories of data we process are:

  • Contact: identifying data (name, surname, telephone, email, and IP address).
  • Commercial communications: identifying data (email).

We do not process special categories of personal data.

 

9. To which recipients will your data be communicated?

In those cases where access to data by third parties is necessary for better management and provision of services, we guarantee the maintenance of confidentiality in the processing of personal data. In any case, these transfers will be adequately communicated to the User, detailing the identity of the Recipient and the purpose thereof. Consequently, we inform you that, in any case, the third parties to whom your data may be communicated are the following according to the purpose:

  • Contact or commercial communications: the following companies will have access to the personal information necessary to carry out their functions as data processors, but may not use it for other purposes. In addition, they must treat personal information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws.
    • Google Analytics: a web analytics service provided by Google, Inc., a Delaware company whose main office is at 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View (California), CA 94043, United States (“Google”). You should know that Google has its servers hosted outside the EU in the US and is covered by the EU-US Privacy Shield agreement, information available here. Google Analytics uses "cookies", which are text files placed on your computer, to help us analyze how users use the website. For more information, see our cookie policy.
    • WhatsApp®: if you use this instant messaging system to communicate with us, you should know that your data will be processed by WhatsApp Ireland Limited. You can find out how this company processes your data here.

 

10. What are your rights when you provide us with your data?

  • Access: You have the right to obtain confirmation as to whether Pablo Urrea is processing your personal data.
  • Rectification: You may modify your data when it is inaccurate.
  • Deletion: You may request the deletion of your data.
  • Objection: You may request that your data not be processed.
  • Limitation of processing: You may request limitation of the processing of your data when:
    • While the accuracy of your data is being checked.
    • When the processing is unlawful and you oppose the deletion of the data.
    • When Pablo Urrea does not need to process your data, but you need it for the exercise or defense of claims.
    • When you have objected to the processing of your data for the satisfaction of a legitimate interest of Pablo Urrea, while verifying whether this interest prevails over your rights and freedoms.
  • Portability: You may receive, in electronic format, the personal data you have provided to us and those obtained from your relationship with Pablo Urrea, as well as have them transmitted to another entity of your choice.
  • Contact channels: paurca@gmail.com.

You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing based on prior consent withdrawal.

If you believe that your request has not been properly addressed, you can contact the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.agpd.es) and file a complaint.

To exercise your rights, please attach a copy of your ID or identification document to your request.

The exercise of these rights is free of charge.

These rights are characterized by the following:

  • Their exercise is free, unless they are manifestly unfounded or excessive requests (e.g., repetitive nature), in which case Pablo Urrea may charge a fee proportionate to the administrative costs incurred or refuse to act.
  • You may exercise your rights directly or through your legal or voluntary representative.
  • We must respond to your request within one month, although, considering the complexity and number of requests, the deadline may be extended by another two months.
  • We are obliged to inform you about the means to exercise these rights, which must be accessible and cannot deny you the exercise of the right solely because you choose another means. If the request is made electronically, the information will be provided electronically where possible, unless you request otherwise.
  • If Pablo Urrea does not respond to the request, it will inform you, no later than one month, of the reasons for not acting and the possibility of complaining to a Control Authority.

 

11. How do we protect your information?

At Pablo Urrea, we are committed to protecting your personal information. We use measures, controls, and procedures of a physical, organizational, and technological nature that are reasonably reliable and effective, aimed at preserving the integrity and security of your data and ensuring its privacy.

In addition, all personnel with access to personal data have been trained and are aware of their obligations regarding the processing of their personal data.

All these security measures are reviewed periodically to ensure their adequacy and effectiveness by our Data Protection Officer.

However, absolute security cannot be guaranteed, and there is no security system that is impregnable, so in the event of any information being processed and under our control being compromised as a result of a security breach, we will take appropriate measures to investigate the incident, notify the Control Authority, and, where appropriate, those users who may have been affected so that they can take appropriate measures.

 

12. Commercial communications by electronic means

Pablo Urrea informs you that it complies with Law 34/2002 of July 11, on Services of the Information Society and Electronic Commerce and does not carry out SPAM practices, therefore, it will request your consent to process your email or mobile phone for commercial purposes at all times.

Pablo Urrea reserves the right to modify its privacy policy or conditions of use of its services for reasons of adaptation to current legislation, or other reasons. When this occurs, we will notify you of any changes and ask you to review the most recent version of our policy and, if necessary, confirm your acceptance. Absolute confidentiality and privacy of the personal data collected are ensured, and measures have therefore been adopted to prevent alteration, loss, treatment, or unauthorized access, thus guaranteeing their integrity, availability, and security. However, Pablo Urrea will not be liable for incidents that may arise regarding personal data when these derive: either from an attack or unauthorized access to the systems in such a way that it is impossible to detect or prevent it, even when measures are taken according to the state of current technology.
